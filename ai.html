AI in Perspective Summary: every slide of the talk, with my commentary.

AI is not the first disruptive technology. The earlier ones left a record of how adoption played out, and that record tells us where AI is headed, what to believe, and what to doubt. The examples I use throughout are the steam engine, the power loom, business computing, internet fiber, and solar power.

Writing

Supervised Drafting and the AI Detector

The paper makes two points about AI detectors.

  1. Relying on a detector’s reported accuracy commits the prosecutor’s fallacy. Vendors report how often AI-written text is flagged, but an accuser needs the chance that a flagged text was written by AI. That chance is about 94% for native English writers and only 20% for non-native ones.
  2. A detector may confuse AI-assisted writing with sole AI authorship. It cannot tell an author who directed and checked an AI’s draft from one who let the AI write alone.
